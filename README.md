# Hollow Vigil

A turn-based dungeon RPG demo for the browser. You are a hired blade locked into an abandoned hill chapel to put
down what is waking beneath it. Every sword stroke is one turn, every roll is shown, and nothing is spent on a level-up
screen: Strength, Dexterity and your sword skill grow from what you actually do in a fight. Wounds land on a body
part, bleeding does not stop on its own, and the best gear in the crypt is too heavy for you until you earn it.

**Play it at https://demidummy.github.io/hollow-vigil/**

Keyboard and mouse. Press H in the game for the controls.

This repo only holds the built game. It's published by `tools/release-pages.sh`.
