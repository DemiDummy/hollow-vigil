# Hollow Vigil

A turn-based dungeon RPG demo for the browser. You are locked into an abandoned hill chapel to put down what is
waking beneath it: as a sellsword, a crossbow-carrying arbalist or a hedge pyromancer. Every stroke, bolt and spell is
one turn, every roll is shown, and nothing is spent on a level-up screen: your attributes, swordsmanship, marksmanship
and fire magic grow from what you actually do in a fight. Wounds land on a body part, bleeding does not stop on its
own, and the best gear in the crypt is too heavy for you until you earn it.

**Play it at https://demidummy.github.io/hollow-vigil/**

Keyboard and mouse. Press H in the game for the controls.

This repo only holds the built game. It's published by `tools/release-pages.sh`.
